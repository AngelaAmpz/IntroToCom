Our Message

Why it matters

Programming is not just about making applications and website works. It is also about creating a technology that people can trust. Careless coding can lead to errors, security problems, and harm to users. Responsible programming helps make technology safer and more reliable to users.

What can we do

We can practice responsible programming by checking our code, protecting confidintiality, debugging, and respecting other people's work. With these small actions, We can make a big difference in creating a safer and reliable technology to users.

Our pledge

I pledge to write code with honesty, responsibility, and care. I will respect users privacy, learn from my mistakes, and create programs that are helpful, safe, and trustworthy.
